## 1. Phase Operational Scope & Objectives

Phase 1 establishes the foundational user and center management capabilities of the membership‑hub platform. The objectives are:

1. **Database foundation** – Create the core tables (`users`, `roles`, `centers`) with all required constraints, indexes, and audit columns.
2. **User lifecycle** – Implement registration (local and social), authentication, and role assignment, ensuring secure password handling, JWT issuance, and RBAC enforcement.
3. **Center lifecycle** – Provide CRUD operations for centers, enforce unique tax identifiers, and enable assignment of center administrators.
4. **Security & compliance** – Apply OWASP Top 10 mitigations (SQLi, XSS, CSRF, CORS), enforce TLS 1.3, and prepare for audit logging.
5. **Documentation** – Generate comprehensive API, data model, and exception handling documentation with traceability to all requirement, data, exception, and architecture tags.
